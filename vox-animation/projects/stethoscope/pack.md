## Script

1. Yeh cheez aaj har doctor ke gale mein latakti hai. Aur isne apne hi inventor ki maut pakdi thi.
2. Paris, eighteen sixteen. Us zamaane mein heartbeat sunne ke liye doctor seedha patient ki chest pe kaan lagate the.
3. Necker Hospital mein René Laennec ke saamne thi ek young lady, heart problem ke saath. Kaan lagana? Bilkul nahi.
4. Toh unhone ek kaagaz ko roll karke tube banaya, ek end uski chest pe rakha, aur dhyaan se suna.
5. Heartbeat pehle se kahin zyada loud thi. Teen saal mein woh tube lakdi ki bani, naam mila: stethoscope.
6. Aur sabse ajeeb baat? Kehte hain, eighteen twenty-six mein isi stethoscope ne pakdi woh bimaari jisne unki jaan li: tuberculosis.
