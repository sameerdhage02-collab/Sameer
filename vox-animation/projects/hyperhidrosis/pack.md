## ElevenLabs voiceover (Multilingual v2)

```
Kuch log AC mein baithe-baithe bhi paseene se bheeg jaate hain. Aur problem sweat glands mein nahi, kahin aur hai. <break time="1.0s" />
Ise kehte hain hyperhidrosis. Ek US study ke hisaab se, har bees mein se ek insaan ise jhelta hai. <break time="1.2s" />
Hamare jism mein do se chaar million sweat glands hain, aur sabse zyada bhare hain hatheliyon aur talvon mein. <break time="1.0s" />
Har gland ka switch ek nerve hai. Hyperhidrosis mein yeh nerves bina wajah full volume pe signal bhejti rehti hain. <break time="1.0s" />
Kyun sirf kuch log? Genes. Ek-tihai se zyada patients ke parivaar mein kisi aur ko bhi yahi hota hai. <break time="1.2s" />
Aur sabse ajeeb baat? Neend mein yeh paseena ruk jaata hai. Glands wahi hain, bas signal shaant ho jaata hai.
```

## Sources
- Doolittle et al. 2016, prevalence 4.8% in the US: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5099353/
- International Hyperhidrosis Society, condition overview (sympathetic over-activity; palms, soles, underarms, face): https://sweathelp.org/about-hyperhidrosis/condition-overview.html
- Sweat gland counts (2-4 million eccrine glands, densest on palms and soles): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweat_gland and https://www.sweathelp.org/about-hyperhidrosis/physiology-of-normal-sweating
- Family history 30-65% / 34.1% (Korean cohort): https://synapse.koreamed.org/articles/1021351 and https://sweathelp.org/about-hyperhidrosis/epidemiology-of-primary-hyperhidrosis.html
- Diagnostic criteria incl. cessation during sleep: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3734871
